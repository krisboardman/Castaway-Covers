Side Panel Attachment
The side panels are sewn to the left and right edges of the main piece. They don't necessarily start at the very front edge — the setback depends on where the diagonal narrows the needed width below the bolt width.
Side Angle Cut
The diagonal cut tapers each side drop from the full width at the backrest to a narrower width at the front. This ensures the cover drapes neatly without excess fabric at the bottom.
Grommet Placement (for Bungee)
4 grommets total: 2 on left side (front & back), 2 on right side (front & back). Bungee runs lengthwise along each side.
Snap Strap Placement (Optional)
2 straps total (left & right). Each strap is 8" long with snaps inset 1" from each end.

Assembly Instructions
  1. Cut main piece (ML × B) from bolt
  2. Cut side panels if needed (SPL × sideEach) from bolt — qty 2
  3. Attach side panels to main piece (if needed):
    • Align panel's front edge at the attachment start point (see Side Panel Attachment for measurement from front edge)
    • Main piece overlaps the side panels in a lap seam — main piece on top, side panel underneath
    • Use material tape to hold in position, then sew the lap seam
  4. Draw diagonal angle cuts on each side and cut (confirm coverage):
    • On each side drop, mark the diagonal start points
    • Draw a straight line from the back edge to the front edge
    • Verify Confirm Coverage width at armrest before cutting
  5. Reinforce seam lines:
    • Check where the diagonal cut crosses or runs near any sewn seams (especially side panel lap seams)
    • Re-stitch or add a reinforcing stitch line along any seam that was weakened by the diagonal cut
  6. Draw wavy pattern around entire cover edge and cut:
    • After cutting, check all seam lines again — re-stitch any that were cut through by the wavy pattern
  7. Sew in gussets:
    • Sew triangular gusset panels into the back flap and both side ends
    • Use a 1″ hem seam to attach each gusset
    • Gussets should fold flat and inward when the cover is draped over the backrest — they replace excess vinyl so the cover drapes smoothly without extra bulk or tightness
  8. Add grommets
  9. Prepare bungee loops
  10. Prepare and install snap straps
  11. Prepare bungees
  12. Add fabric glue to thread ends
  13. Scotch guard ends
