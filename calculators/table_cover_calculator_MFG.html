Table / Table Set / Grill Cover Calculator

Handles three cases with the same math: a plain table (measure width/length to the table edges, height to the tabletop); a table set measured with the chairs tucked in, to the outer chair edges (height to the top of the chair back); or a grill island — measure width and length to the outer edges and measure HEIGHT TO THE TOP OF THE GRILL (or its raised lid). Uniform drape on all four sides. Diagonal corner cuts with snaps at each corner. Bungees wrap around each short end (2 bungees total, 4 grommets).
What are you covering?
Measured Dimensions
The math is identical for all three — only the measuring guidance and drape choice differ. Height (H) drives the drape depth; F2BS is only needed for the "Over the seats" drape.
Drape Depth
Drape = 10". Cover hangs a short skirt just below the tabletop / outer edge.
Drape = H − F2BS + 5, using the F2BS field in Measured Dimensions. Cover hangs 5" below the seats.
Drape = H − 3. Cover hangs to about 3" off the floor.
Layout & Seams
Grommet & Bungee Placement
Grommets & Bungees
G1 & G3 are on the front drape near the left and right short-end corners; G2 & G4 mirror them on the back drape. Left bungee: G1→G3 (wraps left short end). Right bungee: G2→G4 (wraps right short end).
Corner Snaps
Diagonal cut at all 4 corners. The two adjacent drape flaps fold together along the cut and are held closed by a snap set (1 stud + 1 socket per corner).
