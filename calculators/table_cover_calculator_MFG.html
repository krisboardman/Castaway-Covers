Table Cover Calculator

Single-panel rectangular cover with diagonal corner cuts, corner snaps, and bungees that wrap each end (short side).
Inputs
Recommended drop for tables is around 8–10" — enough to shed water past the edge and protect the upper legs without dragging on the ground or hitting chair seats.
Grommets & Bungees
4 corner grommets (one at each corner of the long sides), set in from the outer edge by drop ÷ 3 so they hang about two-thirds of the way down the side of the table. Two bungees, one at each short end, run through the two corner grommets and wrap under the short side of the table to cinch the cover.
Corner Snaps
A diagonal cut at each corner removes the outer triangle and leaves a triangular flap. The two adjacent side drops fold together at 45°, and snaps hold the seam closed.

Assembly Instructions
  1. Cut the cover panel from the bolt. If the cover width (MD) fits within the bolt width, cut a single ML × MD rectangle. If MD exceeds the bolt width, cut two pieces running front-to-back — each piece is MD long (along the bolt) × ((ML + 1.5)/2) wide — and join them along the long centerline with a 1.5" overlapped seam to form the full ML × MD panel.
  2. Mark and cut the diagonal at each corner: from a point one side-drop in along the long edge to a point one side-drop in along the short edge. Remove the outer triangle. A triangular flap (the inner half of the corner square) remains.
  3. Add the wavy edge pattern around the perimeter (skip the diagonal cut edges, since they will be joined to each other).
  4. At each corner, fold the two adjacent side drops together so the diagonal cut edges meet. Install 2 snaps per corner (add another pair for every ~15" of drop).
  5. Install corner grommets at the four corners where the long sides meet the short sides. On the flat cover, position each grommet drop ÷ 3 in from the outer long edge (and 5" past the diagonal chamfer). When the cover is installed, that puts the grommet about two-thirds of the way down the drop, so the bungee has enough fabric below it to cinch tight underneath without lifting the grommet back over the top edge.
  6. Install the cover by laying it over the table top, snapping the four corners closed, then running a bungee through the two grommets on each short end and wrapping it under the short side of the table to cinch the cover tight.
