Front Flap & Back Piece
The back piece is always required — it covers the back of the chair from backrest top to floor. The front flap is added if the bolt width doesn't cover the full front-to-back distance.
Side Angle Cut
The diagonal is inset from both ends — it only tapers in the middle zone. The back zone (seam area) and front zone (front skirt) stay at constant width. This gives uniform floor clearance along the entire side.
Grommet Placement (for Bungee)
4 grommets total: 2 on left side (front & back), 2 on right side (front & back). Bungee runs lengthwise along each side.
Snap Placement (Back Corners)
One snap pair per back corner — both halves on the back piece. The back piece's side extension folds/pleats at the corner, and the snap closes the fold. Main piece has no snap attachment.
Assembly Instructions
  1. Cut main piece (ML × B) from bolt — runs side-to-side
  2. Cut back piece (trapezoid: ML+seam top, WB+2×hem bottom, H−FC tall) from bolt
  3. Cut front flap (if needed) from bolt
  4. Draw inset diagonal cuts on each side — see "Side Angle Cut" card & flat layout diagram for exact inch values and points A → B:
    • Point A (cut starts): Measure back inset from back edge — mark at the side edge (full width, no trim)
    • Point B (cut ends): Measure front inset from front edge — mark trim distance inward from each side edge
    • Draw a straight diagonal from A → B on each side
    • From Point B to front edge: cut straight vertical at the trim mark (constant front width)
    • Cut along the diagonals and vertical trim lines on both sides
  5. Draw wavy pattern on all outer edges and cut:
    • Apply wavy pattern to the perimeter edges of each piece — floor edges, side edges, and diagonal-cut edges
    • Leave seam edges straight (top of back piece, top of front flap, and the matching edges on the main piece where they attach)
    • Note: corners where wavy edges meet seam edges need a clean transition — taper the wavy pattern to straight near the seam overlap zone
  6. Sew — attach back piece to main piece:
    • Align the trapezoidal back piece along the top (backrest) edge of the main piece — the wider top edge (ML + seam) attaches here
    • Overlap the main piece's 1" hem at the back edge — main piece on top, back piece underneath
    • Use material tape to hold in position, then sew the lap seam. The back piece tapers narrower toward the floor.
  7. Sew — attach front flap to main piece (if needed):
    • Align the front flap along the front edge of the main piece
    • Overlap the seam allowance (1.5") — main piece on top, flap underneath
    • Use material tape to hold in position, then sew the lap seam
  8. Install snaps at back corners (on the back piece only):
    • At each back corner the extension flap folds back on itself (pleats) to take up excess material. A single snap pair closes the fold — both halves sit on the back piece.
    • Install 1 snap pair per corner (2 pairs total):
      • Back-side half: halfway down the back piece, 2" past the WB/2 fold line
      • Front-side half: 2" above the seam with the main piece, (H−FC)/2 in from the outer diagonal corner
    • See the Snap Placement card for exact dimensions based on your chair/couch inputs
  9. Add grommets
  10. Prepare bungee loops
  11. Install snap-strap snaps on the cover (only when customer ordered snap straps):
    • 2 straps total — sew 4 snap halves to the cover (half on the back panel, half on the front skirt at the same horizontal positions)
    • Horizontal positions from center: ±15"
    • Back snap: 2" toward back-floor from the backrest top (= just inside the backrest crease)
    • Front snap: 22" down the front from the backrest top (⅔ × front draping length)
    • Provide 8" webbing straps with a snap on each end (1 per pair). Customer routes each strap from a back snap, over the backrest top, to the matching front snap so the front skirt folds up against the backrest face.
  12. Prepare bungees
  13. Add fabric glue to thread ends
  14. Scotch guard ends
