Side Angle Cut
The diagonal cut is a straight line connecting three points: the front edge, the armrest point (at "Confirm Coverage" width), and the back edge (at MD - BR width). The calculator uses linear interpolation to determine where this diagonal line intersects the front edge of your bolt-width material.
Assembly Instructions
  1. Cut main piece
  2. Cut side pieces if needed
  3. Cut out gusset corner
  4. Sew on sides:
    • Use clips to contain main piece
    • Use plastic to slide material
    • Use material tape to hold pieces together at 1/2" hem ea. piece
  5. Draw angles and cut (confirm coverage)
  6. Draw waves and cut
  7. Add grommets
  8. Prepare bungee loops
  9. Prepare and install snap straps (must add before adding gussets)
  10. Cut and sew on gussets / bungee loop (on gusset side)
  11. Prepare bungees
  12. Add fabric glue to thread ends
  13. Scotch guard ends of back
