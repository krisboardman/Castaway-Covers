Front Flap & Back Piece
The back piece is always required — it covers the back of the chair from backrest top to floor. The front flap is added if the bolt width doesn't cover the full front-to-back distance.
Side Angle Cut
The diagonal is inset from both ends — it only tapers in the middle zone. The back zone (gusset/seam area) and front zone (front skirt) stay at constant width. This gives uniform floor clearance along the entire side.
Grommet Placement (for Bungee)
4 grommets total: 2 on left side (front & back), 2 on right side (front & back). Bungee runs lengthwise along each side.
Snap Strap Placement (Optional)
2 straps total (left & right). Each strap is 8" long with snaps inset 1" from each end.

Assembly Instructions
  1. Cut main piece (ML × B) from bolt — runs side-to-side
  2. Cut back piece (backPieceLength × backPieceWidth) from bolt
  3. Cut front flap (if needed) from bolt
  4. Draw inset diagonal cuts on each side — see "Side Angle Cut" card & flat layout diagram for exact inch values and points A → B:
    • Point A (cut starts): Measure back inset from back edge — mark at the side edge (full width, no trim)
    • Point B (cut ends): Measure front inset from front edge — mark trim distance inward from each side edge
    • Draw a straight diagonal from A → B on each side
    • From Point B to front edge: cut straight vertical at the trim mark (constant front width)
    • Cut along the diagonals and vertical trim lines on both sides
  5. Draw wavy pattern on all outer edges and cut:
    • Apply wavy pattern to the perimeter edges of each piece — floor edges, side edges, and diagonal-cut edges
    • Leave seam edges straight (top of back piece, top of front flap, and the matching edges on the main piece where they attach)
    • Note: corners where wavy edges meet seam edges need a clean transition — taper the wavy pattern to straight near the seam overlap zone
  6. Sew — attach back piece to main piece:
    • Align the back piece along the top (backrest) edge of the main piece
    • Overlap the main piece's 1" hem at the back edge — main piece on top, back piece underneath
    • This is the same 1" hem zone used for gusset attachment — use material tape to hold in position, then sew the lap seam
  7. Sew — attach front flap to main piece (if needed):
    • Align the front flap along the front edge of the main piece
    • Overlap the seam allowance (1.5") — main piece on top, flap underneath
    • Use material tape to hold in position, then sew the lap seam
  8. Sew in gussets:
    • Sew triangular gusset panels into the back flap and both side ends
    • Use a 1″ hem seam to attach each gusset
    • Gussets should fold flat and inward when the cover is draped over the backrest — they replace excess vinyl so the cover drapes smoothly without extra bulk or tightness
  9. Add grommets
  10. Prepare bungee loops
  11. Prepare and install snap straps
  12. Prepare bungees
  13. Add fabric glue to thread ends
  14. Scotch guard ends
