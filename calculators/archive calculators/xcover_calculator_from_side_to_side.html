Side Angle Cut
The diagonal cut tapers each side drop from the full bolt width at armrest height to a narrower depth at the floor. This ensures the cover drapes neatly without excess fabric pooling at the bottom while maintaining 3" floor clearance on the back and sides.
Assembly Instructions
  1. Cut main piece (B × MW) from bolt
  2. Cut back piece (WB × BPL) from bolt
  3. Cut front piece if needed (FPW × FPL) from bolt — FPW = W + 2(F2A−FC)
  4. Position main piece over chair (side-to-side):
    • Center the fabric left-to-right over the chair
    • Drape over left arm, across seat, over right arm
    • Extend back portion up inside back and over backrest
  5. Attach back piece (shingle lap seam):
    • Back piece goes UNDER the main piece at backrest top
    • Overlap is on the back piece (+1.5" seam allowance)
    • Use material tape to hold at overlap, then sew
  6. Attach front piece if needed (shingle lap seam):
    • Front piece goes UNDER the main piece at seat front
    • Overlap is on the front piece (+1.5" seam allowance)
    • Use material tape to hold at overlap, then sew
  7. Draw diagonal angle cuts on each side drop and cut (confirm coverage):
    • On each side drop's bottom edge, mark the diagonal start point
    • Draw a straight line from the mark to the front-top corner
    • Verify Confirm Coverage depth at bottom before cutting
  8. Draw waves and cut
  9. Add grommets
  10. Prepare bungee loops
  11. Prepare and install snap straps
  12. Prepare bungees
  13. Add fabric glue to thread ends
  14. Scotch guard ends
