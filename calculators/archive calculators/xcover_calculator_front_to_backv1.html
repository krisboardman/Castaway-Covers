Side Angle Cut
The diagonal cut tapers each side drop from the full width at the backrest to a narrower width at the front. This ensures the cover drapes neatly without excess fabric at the bottom.
Side Panel Attachment
The side panels are sewn to the left and right edges of the main piece. They don't necessarily start at the very front edge — the setback depends on where the diagonal narrows the needed width below the bolt width.

Assembly Instructions
  1. Cut main piece (ML × B) from bolt
  2. Cut side panels if needed (SPL × sideEach) from bolt — qty 2
  3. Attach side panels to main piece (if needed):
    • Align panel's back edge with the attachment start point (see Side Panel Attachment)
    • Sew along the left/right edges of the main piece
    • Use material tape to hold at seam overlap, then sew
  4. Position assembled main piece over chair (front-to-back):
    • Back edge drapes down the back of the chair to floor clearance
    • Material goes up and over the backrest
    • Drapes diagonally across seat
    • Front portion drapes down front face to floor
    • Sides wrap over both arms and hang down
  5. Draw diagonal angle cuts on each side and cut (confirm coverage):
    • On each side drop, mark the diagonal start points
    • Draw a straight line from the back edge to the front edge
    • Verify Confirm Coverage width at armrest before cutting
  6. Draw waves and cut
  7. Add grommets
  8. Prepare bungee loops
  9. Prepare and install snap straps
  10. Prepare bungees
  11. Add fabric glue to thread ends
  12. Scotch guard ends
