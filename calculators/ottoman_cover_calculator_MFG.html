Ottoman Cover Calculator

Single-panel rectangular cover with diagonal corner cuts, corner snaps, and bungees that wrap each end (short side). Material drops all the way to the ground (height − floor clearance).
Inputs
Side drop is computed as H − FC, so the cover hangs to within the floor clearance. Measure to the top of the cushion if your ottoman has one. Note: if your cushion exceeds 2″ in thickness and you use the cover on the ottoman without the cushion, the cover may drag on the ground.
Grommets & Bungees
4 corner grommets (one at each corner of the long sides), set in from the outer edge by drop ÷ 3 so they hang about two-thirds of the way down the side of the ottoman. Two bungees, one at each short end, run through the two corner grommets and wrap under the short side of the ottoman to cinch the cover.
Corner Snaps
A diagonal cut at each corner removes the outer triangle and leaves a triangular flap. The two adjacent side drops fold together at 45°, and snaps hold the seam closed.
