# Castaway Covers — Pricing Assumptions

_Agreed October 23, 2026. These drive the website rates and the profit calculator defaults._

## Your time per piece (hours)

| Piece | Measuring (incl. travel) | Design | Build | Installation | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chair, sofa, chaise lounge | 0.5 | 0.5 | 1 | 0.5 | 2.5 |
| Table, ottoman, table set | 0.5 | 0.5 | 1 | 0.5 | 2.5 |
| Sectional (custom quote) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 7 |
| Grill island (custom quote) | 1 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 9 |

- Basic pieces get 0.5 hours of design because you already have designs for them.
- Installation time is set to match measuring time.
- The sectional's 2 hours of design is an estimate. The grill island's 3 hours of design comes from your recent job.

## Cost inputs

- Your labor: **$50/hr**
- Manufacturer labor: **$0** (you do all the manufacturing)
- Vinyl: **$15.95/yd**
- Hardware: about **$8–10 per cover** (bungees, cord locks, hooks, grommets, snaps)
- NJ sales tax: not counted as a cost (it's collected from the customer on top of the price)

## Margin target

- Target **50%**, with a floor of **40%** (margin = profit ÷ selling price)

## Multi-piece orders

Each rate includes measuring, design and installation for one piece. In a set, measuring and design happen once, so extra matching pieces cost you about 1 hour ($50) less each. Discount multi-piece orders on the final invoice. The website tells customers this.
